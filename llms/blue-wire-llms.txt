# Blue Wire

> Blue Wire is a sports podcast network and creator-monetization platform. It provides ad
> sales, sponsorship coordination, marketing, distribution, and operational support to a
> network of independent sports podcasters, so creators produce content while Blue Wire
> sells and services the advertising around it. Backed by 500 Global.

This file was generated by API Evangelist from the public Blue Wire website on 2026-08-10.
It is not published by Blue Wire.

## Important

Blue Wire publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-10 there is no developer portal, no
API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, and no `/.well-known/`
discovery surface. `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.`, `app.` and `studio.` subdomains of
bluewirepods.com do not resolve. `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`, `/api`, `/developers` and
`/docs` all return 404 on the apex host. The site is a Webflow-published marketing and
media property; the business is media and advertising, not software.
